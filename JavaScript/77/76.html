Fun Facts!

  • Mr. Potato Head celebrates his birthday in 1952, the year he went on sale!
  • He started out as just face parts you could stick on a real potato.
  • People have made giant versions of him for fun displays.
  • Over 60 million Mr. Potato Head toys have been sold worldwide.
  • You can mix and match pieces to make silly faces.
